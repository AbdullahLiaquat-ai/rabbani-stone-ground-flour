Traditional Stone Milling

Steel roller mills spin fast and heat the grain, stripping away the germ and much of its nutrition. Our chakki stones turn slowly, milling at low temperatures so the natural oils, fibre and flavour of the wheat stay intact.

Solar-Powered Milling

We run a solar-assisted grinding line, cutting diesel dependence and keeping the mill running on sunlight for most of the day — quieter, cleaner, and true to the traditional process.
